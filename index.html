Research

Can Inherent Graph Structure Enhance In-Context Learning?
Yifei Sun, Siyuan Gong, Yuxiang Wan, Zaoshi Wang, Haoyang Zhong, Chunping Wang, Lei CHEN, Yang Yang
Submitted to KDD 2025  ·  Preprint, 2025
One of the most important experiences of my life — it ignited my passion for research and made me rethink my future plans! We propose ContextG, the first framework to introduce graph structures into ICL. By modeling structural relationships among examples and test queries, ContextG enhances LLM reasoning while improving efficiency.
